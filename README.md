One sentence pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable — NapAlarm, the
alarm that wakes you five minutes before your alarm — entered in the
make.fun competition "The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched".

Bounty page: https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23

## The pitch

> NapAlarm is the alarm that wakes you five minutes before your alarm, so you can lie there dreading the real one in peace — the only alarm clock engineered to make mornings worse, and proud of it.

## How it works

`main.py` is pure Python standard library. It writes the pitch to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). The output is
byte-identical on every run.
